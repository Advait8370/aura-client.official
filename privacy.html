1. About Aura Launcher

Aura Launcher is a desktop application designed to help users manage supported Minecraft clients and related game-launching tasks.

In this policy, "Aura Launcher", "we", and "our" refer to the people or organization responsible for distributing the application. "You" refers to anyone using it.

2. Information the Application May Process

Depending on the features you use and the application's implementation, Aura Launcher may process information such as:

  • Local application information: settings, selected installation directories, client configuration, and application preferences.
  • Device and environment information: operating system details, Java installation paths, and information needed to launch supported clients.
  • Authentication information: account-related tokens or session information handled by the authentication flow, if you choose to sign in.
  • Diagnostic information: error details or logs generated when a launch, installation, update, or other operation fails.

This list describes possible categories, not a claim that every category is collected or transmitted. The actual information processed depends on the version and features you use.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the application may be used where necessary to:

  • Run the launcher and remember your settings.
  • Manage supported game clients and local files.
  • Complete authentication that you initiate.
  • Check for application updates and retrieve configuration.
  • Identify and troubleshoot technical errors.
  • Protect the application and its supported services.

Any additional use, including analytics or advertising, should be described here if it is implemented.

4. Local Files and Game Directories

Features such as client installation, repair, deletion, and game launching may read or modify files in the directories you select or that the application uses.

Review file-operation prompts carefully and maintain backups of important game data. The exact files accessed depend on the features you use and your configuration.

5. Microsoft Authentication

If you use a Microsoft sign-in feature, authentication may involve Microsoft and other services required for the relevant Minecraft account or game entitlement. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Do not enter your Microsoft password into an unofficial form. Before publishing this section, verify the actual authentication flow, token storage, permissions requested, and whether any account data is transmitted to the launcher developer.

6. Updates and Third-Party Services

Aura Launcher may contact third-party services to retrieve releases, application update metadata, client configuration, or files required by its features.

For example, if configured in your installed version, GitHub-hosted repositories or releases may be used to distribute updates or client configuration. Requests to those services may expose normal connection information, such as your IP address, to the relevant service provider.

Third-party services have their own data practices. Review their policies before using integrations that require an account or transmit information.

This section must reflect the application's verified behavior. Document whether the application sends data to the developer, analytics providers, crash-reporting services, advertising providers, or other recipients.

8. Data Storage and Security

Application settings, configuration, logs, and authentication-related data may be stored locally or handled by integrated services, depending on the implementation.

The final published policy should specify where relevant data is stored, how long it is retained, how credentials and tokens are protected, and what steps users can take to remove local data.

No software or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

The retention period for local files, logs, account information, and any remotely stored data depends on how each feature is implemented.

To request deletion of information held by the developer, use the contact details below. This request cannot directly remove data held independently by Microsoft, GitHub, or another third-party service.

10. Children's Privacy

Aura Launcher is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to a service operated by the developer, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control certain personal information.

You can also choose whether to use optional features such as account sign-in. Available choices may be limited by technical requirements or legal obligations. Contact us for questions about applicable rights.
